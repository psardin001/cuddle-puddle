Cuddle Puddle privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Cuddle Puddle is a game by MrFourtacci. This policy covers the game on every platform it is released on (Steam, Google Play, the App Store).

What the game collects

Nothing. Cuddle Puddle has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it never connects to the internet. It doesn't collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data.

What stays on your device

Your save and your settings (language, volume, display options) are small files kept on your own device, in the game's private storage. They never leave it, unless your platform backs them up for you: for example Steam Cloud on PC, which Valve runs under its own privacy policy. Uninstalling the game deletes them on phones.

The stores

When you buy or download the game, the store you use (Steam, Google Play, the App Store) handles payment and may collect data under its own privacy policy. The developer receives only the sales reports those stores give every developer, with no personal details.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If the game ever starts collecting anything, this page will say what and why before that version is released, and the date above will change.
